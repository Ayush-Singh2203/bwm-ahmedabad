WIRE MESH WEAVING STYLES

Plain Weave

The most common and versatile pattern — ideal for general-purpose applications where uniform openings and consistent strength are required.

Twill Weave

Offers enhanced strength and finer filtration. Each wire passes alternately over and under two wires, creating a diagonal pattern perfect for heavy-duty or high-precision uses.

Dutch Weave

Features warp wires of larger diameter woven tightly with finer weft wires. Provides very fine filtration widely used in liquid and gas filtration applications.
